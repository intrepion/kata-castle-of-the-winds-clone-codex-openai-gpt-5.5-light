# Use a four-floor dungeon arc

The first shipped dungeon will be four floors deep with a final objective rather than a single floor or endless procedural descent. Four floors are enough to create escalation, recovery decisions, and a win condition while staying small enough for browser verification and iteration.

**Consequences**

Procedural generation, monster tables, loot, and quest beats should support a beginning, middle, and end. Endless play can come later, but the initial game needs a finishable run.
