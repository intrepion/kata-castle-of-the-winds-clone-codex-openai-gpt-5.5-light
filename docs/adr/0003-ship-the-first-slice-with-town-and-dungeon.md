# Ship the first slice with Town and Dungeon

Windglass Keep's first playable slice will include both a thin Town and a thin Dungeon instead of starting entirely underground. The preparation-and-return rhythm is part of the Castle-style loop, so even a minimal Town is more truthful than a larger dungeon without a safe hub.

**Consequences**

The first build must support at least one usable Town preparation flow, dungeon entry, dungeon exit or return, and browser-local continuation. Town can be shallow at first, but it cannot be merely decorative.
