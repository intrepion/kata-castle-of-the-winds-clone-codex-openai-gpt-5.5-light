# Use original Windglass lore

Windglass Keep will use its own strange keep, wind, and glass motif rather than staying close to the source game's Norse framing. The game should preserve the interaction grammar and RPG loop of its inspiration while letting names, lore, monsters, and artifacts stand on their own.

**Consequences**

Content should avoid source-game proper nouns and mythology-specific duplication. The setting can feel old, eerie, and shareware-fantasy-adjacent without depending on recognizable borrowed lore.
