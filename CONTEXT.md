# Windglass Keep

Windglass Keep is an original browser dungeon crawler inspired by Castle of the Winds. Its language preserves the feel of a mouse-first Windows shareware RPG while naming this game on its own terms.

## Language

**Adventurer**:
The player character exploring town and dungeon spaces. The Adventurer has a class, attributes, equipment, carried items, spells, health, mana, gold, and quest progress.
_Avoid_: Player avatar, hero, character

**Sentinel**:
An Adventurer class built around durability, steady melee attacks, and forgiving early survival.
_Avoid_: Warrior, fighter, tank

**Arcanist**:
An Adventurer class built around mana, spell utility, and high-risk control of encounters.
_Avoid_: Wizard, mage, caster

**Wayfarer**:
An Adventurer class built around mobility, awareness, ranged pressure, and practical dungeon survival.
_Avoid_: Rogue, ranger, scout

**Town**:
The safe hub where the Adventurer prepares, shops, identifies or sells treasure, recovers, and advances quest context before returning below ground.
_Avoid_: Lobby, overworld, menu

**Rest**:
A Town action that restores the Adventurer enough to continue the current run without adding tactical dungeon time.
_Avoid_: Sleep, heal button, recovery

**The Keep**:
The four-floor dungeon beneath Town, framed as a strange wind-and-glass ruin with a final objective.
_Avoid_: Castle, tower, dungeon

**Dungeon Floor**:
A single generated underground level made of rooms, corridors, doors, traps, monsters, treasure, stairs, and fog of war.
_Avoid_: Map, level, board

**Turn**:
The atomic unit of dungeon time. Adventurer actions and monster responses advance through turns.
_Avoid_: Tick, frame, step

**Roll**:
A visible game calculation recorded in the Log when uncertainty matters, such as attacks, defenses, damage, detection, or spell effects.
_Avoid_: RNG, proc, hidden check

**Encounter**:
A tactical situation on a Dungeon Floor involving one or more monsters, the Adventurer, and the nearby terrain or loot that changes the risk calculation.
_Avoid_: Fight, battle, combat scene

**Spell**:
A learned magical action that spends mana to change a dungeon or encounter state, such as harming a monster, escaping danger, detecting hidden threats, or improving survival.
_Avoid_: Skill, ability, power

**Spark**:
A direct-damage Spell for harming a visible monster.
_Avoid_: Firebolt, magic missile

**Mend**:
A recovery Spell for restoring the Adventurer during danger or between encounters.
_Avoid_: Heal, cure

**Ward**:
A protection Spell for reducing incoming danger or improving short-term survival.
_Avoid_: Shield, armor spell

**Reveal**:
A detection Spell for exposing hidden threats, secrets, or tactical information.
_Avoid_: Detect, scan

**Blink**:
An escape Spell for short repositioning when an encounter turns dangerous.
_Avoid_: Teleport, dash

**Glassbind**:
A control Spell for slowing, rooting, or otherwise limiting a monster through the Windglass motif.
_Avoid_: Freeze, snare

**Supply**:
A mundane consumable or preparation item bought in Town and carried into The Keep.
_Avoid_: Potion, provision, shop item

**Pack**:
The Adventurer's carried item collection, constrained by practical capacity and separate from equipped gear.
_Avoid_: Inventory, bag, stash

**Pack Slot**:
A single capacity unit in the Pack. Items consume Pack Slots rather than weight.
_Avoid_: Weight, burden, encumbrance point

**Paper Doll**:
The equipment view that represents gear by body or hand slot, preserving the desktop RPG metaphor instead of treating equipment as a generic list.
_Avoid_: Loadout, equipment panel, gear screen

**Main Hand**:
The Paper Doll slot for the Adventurer's primary weapon or active implement.
_Avoid_: Weapon slot, right hand

**Off Hand**:
The Paper Doll slot for a shield, secondary weapon, or supporting implement.
_Avoid_: Shield slot, left hand

**Charm**:
A Paper Doll slot for one small magical keepsake or protective object.
_Avoid_: Trinket, accessory, talisman

**Guard**:
A monster behavior that holds territory and fights predictably when approached.
_Avoid_: Basic AI, stationary monster

**Hunter**:
A monster behavior that pursues the Adventurer aggressively after detection.
_Avoid_: Chaser, aggro enemy

**Skulker**:
A monster behavior that uses hiding, distance, or opportunistic movement to make encounters uncertain.
_Avoid_: Sneak, rogue enemy

**Hexer**:
A monster behavior that threatens the Adventurer through spells, curses, or other ranged pressure.
_Avoid_: Caster enemy, mage monster

**Log**:
The visible running record of dungeon events, rolls, warnings, discoveries, and quest beats.
_Avoid_: Console, feed, transcript

**Journal**:
The persistent record of discovered lore, quest state, help text, and memorable events across play sessions.
_Avoid_: Codex, notebook, quest log

**Save Slot**:
A browser-local saved game position that can be continued later without a server account.
_Avoid_: Profile, checkpoint, cloud save

**Windglass**:
The game's recurring material and mood: brittle, luminous, old, and strange, distinct from the source inspiration's lore.
_Avoid_: Norse myth, generic magic crystal
